About CryptoCalc

CryptoCalc is a free educational calculator that helps visitors estimate cryptocurrency position size, margin, stop-loss exposure, take-profit outcomes and risk-to-reward ratios.

The calculator uses values entered by the visitor. It does not connect to exchanges, execute trades, provide live prices or recommend buying or selling an asset.

Our purpose

We aim to make basic trade-planning calculations easier to understand. Always verify calculations and trading conditions with your exchange.

Editorial approach

CryptoCalc focuses on practical, beginner-friendly explanations of trade-planning concepts. We aim to explain assumptions, show calculation steps and distinguish estimates from actual exchange results. Educational pages are reviewed for clarity, and corrections are welcome through our Contact page.

How information is prepared

Our explanations are written to describe general concepts and simplified calculations in plain language. They are not a substitute for exchange documentation, professional advice or independent verification. We welcome corrections when a calculation or explanation is unclear.

CryptoCalc is an independent educational website and is not affiliated with any cryptocurrency exchange, broker or token project.
