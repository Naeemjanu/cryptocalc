How to use this calculator

  • Choose LONG if you expect price to rise, or SHORT if you expect it to fall.
  • Enter your available margin and leverage.
  • Set your entry, stop-loss and take-profit prices.
  • Review estimated loss, profit and risk-to-reward before trading.

Important risk reminder

  • Leverage increases exposure and can cause rapid losses.
  • Never risk money you cannot afford to lose.
  • Use isolated margin if appropriate for your strategy.
  • Check your exchange's liquidation price and fee schedule separately.
